Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026.

Who we are

NutriiApp is a simple app for logging your meals and seeing your day’s nutrition at a glance. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, contact us at hello@gr8m.com.

Information we collect

We collect the minimum needed to run the app. Everything below is given by you when you use the app, except where noted:

  • Account information: your email address and your username. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and the name on your Google account instead of a chosen username.
  • Password: if you sign up with email, your password goes to our sign-in provider and is stored scrambled so it cannot be read back, not even by us. If you sign in with Google, we never see or store a password for you.
  • Meals you log: what you ate, how much, the type of meal, and the day it belongs to. This is the content of the app: without it there is nothing to show you.
  • Sign-in session (automatic): your browser keeps a token so you stay signed in between visits. Signing out removes it.
  • Font request (automatic): pages load a font from Google Fonts, which sees a normal web request when pages load, like any website resource.

We do not collect your location, your contacts, your photos, or anything else from your device. There are no ad trackers and no analytics tools watching how you use the app.

How we use it

Your information is used only to run the app for you:

  • Creating and protecting your account, and signing you in and out.
  • Saving the meals you log and showing them back to you with your daily nutrition.
  • Sending password-reset emails when you ask for one.
  • Keeping your meals visible only to your account.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers named below.

Where it is kept and how it is protected

Everything you enter is stored in our database, hosted by Supabase. Access is restricted so that your meals can only be read or changed by your own signed-in account. Passwords are never stored in readable form. Like any internet service, no method of storage is perfectly secure, but we limit collection to what the app needs and restrict access to your own data.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party, except:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database and sign-in service and stores your information on our behalf (supabase.com/privacy).
  • Google, but only if you choose Google sign-in: that login step goes through Google, and Google’s handling of it is covered by their own policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

How long we keep it

We keep your account and meals for as long as your account exists, so the app keeps working for you. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your sign-in, your username, and all of your meals.

Your choices and rights

  • See your data: your dashboard and profile show what we hold about you.
  • Correct it: you can change your username on the profile page.
  • Sign out: the account menu signs you out on that browser.
  • Delete everything: email hello@gr8m.com from the address you signed up with and ask for deletion.

Children

NutriiApp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at hello@gr8m.com and we will delete it.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions about privacy, or requests about your data, go to hello@gr8m.com. Also see our terms of use.
